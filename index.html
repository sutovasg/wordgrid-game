How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be five letters long. The colours tell you how close you are.

PLANT
P is in the word and in the right spot.
CRANE
R is in the word but in a different spot.
WAVES
V is not in the word, or is an extra occurrence.

A new puzzle is available every day. Or switch to Practice for unlimited rounds. Settings include hard mode and colour-blind friendly colours.
